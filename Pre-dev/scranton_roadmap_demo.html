Phase 01 — Research guide: how to listen
01
Screener form
3 questions max via LinkedIn DM or Typeform. No product description, no name. Select for variety: remote, hybrid, on-site.
02
1:1 interviews — 30 min
Always 1:1, never focus groups first. Record with permission. Listen more than you write. Never mention Scranton.
03
Concept validation
Only after 8+ interviews. Show slides to 3–4 best participants. Watch their eyes, not their words.
04
Focus group — optional
4–6 mixed participants after concept validation. Tests social dynamics. How does a manager react with a team member in the room?
Golden signal: if someone says "can I be in the beta?" before you've finished the sentence, that's your product-market fit signal. Note it verbatim. Never ask "Would you use this?" — everyone says yes. Ask "When would you open it?" and "What would make you stop?"
