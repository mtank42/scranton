Vision
AN OS,
NOT AN
AI COACH
Scranton is a managerial operating system. Cold data (time, workload, projects) meets warm data (morale, weak signals, equity) in a unified, ethical, sovereign interface.

AI is never visible as a decision-maker. It structures, summarises, detects — silently. The manager remains the sole source of every human decision.

"AI should simplify, not decide. The manager remains the human who calls the shots — Scranton gives them the time and data to do it better."
