02 / PROBLEM

Proximity management is exhausting — and underserved

01

Meeting overload

Preparing agendas, running meetings, writing minutes, tracking actions: managers spend more time documenting than actually managing. Meetings get longer. Actions get lost.

02

The human blind spot

Fatigue, disengagement, latent tension: weak signals are never formally captured. Burnout is discovered when it's already too late to act.

03

Tools built for HR, not managers

Workday, HRIS, spreadsheets: built for compliance reporting, not for the manager face to face with their team every day. Zero decision support.

"AI should simplify, not decide. The manager remains the human who calls the shots — Michael Scott gives them the time and data to do it better."

03 / Vision

AN OS,
NOT AN
AI COACH

Scranton is a managerial operating system. Cold data (time, workload, projects) meets warm data (morale, weak signals, equity) in a unified, ethical, sovereign interface.

AI is never visible as a decision-maker. It structures, summarises, detects — silently. The manager remains the sole source of every human decision.

Core principles
▸ 01

Tell Dwight — Universal Input

One free-text field. Dwight routes each note where it belongs, feeding multiple files simultaneously. Asks follow-up questions if your note is too vague.

▸ 02

Ask Pam — Collective Memory

Pam knows everything ever said, decided, filed, or promised. Ask a question, get a briefing. Ask her to draft — she drafts from memory. Written or spoken answers.

▸ 03

Whisper Jim — Ephemeral Chat

The coffee machine, digitised. Conversations are ephemeral by default — no AI, no indexing. Unless your team unanimously decides otherwise.

▸ 04

Team moodboard + Fewer meetings

Daily mood check-in. AI-generated agendas. Automated minutes. Remote, hybrid, on-site.

▸ 05

Privacy by design

Local-first. Names never leave your machine before anonymisation. Your data, not HR's.

09 / WHISPER JIM

Whisper Jim — the coffee machine, digitised

Ephemeral by design. Indexed only when your team unanimously decides. The anti-noise RAG.
Core principles
01
Ephemeral by default
Conversations are not processed by AI and don't feed Ask Pam. What's said at the coffee machine stays at the coffee machine. Text and voice.
02
Emotional safety
The space for raw warm data — doubts, jokes, frustration — without fear that an AI draws premature conclusions from it. Gen Z and beyond: voice-first, no trace by default.
03
"Pam needs to know this?"
If a conversation produces a decision worth keeping, any participant triggers a vote. Unanimous consent only. If everyone agrees → Invite Dwight → he reads, cleans, routes to the right files. One participant validates before filing.
The RAG advantage. Slack indexes everything — including noise. Whisper Jim keeps Ask Pam's memory pure, dense, and qualified: fed only by what the team consciously chose to remember.
